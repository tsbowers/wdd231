Trail Preparation Guide

A little preparation can make your hiking experience safe, more enjoyable, and more comfortable.

What to Bring

  • Water bottle or hydration pack
  • Trail snacks
  • Comfortable hiking shoes
  • Small first aid kit
  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Sunscreen and bug spray
  • Phone or trail map

Before You Go

  • Check weather conditions
  • Tell someone your plans
  • Research trail difficulty
  • Bring enough water
  • Charge your phone
  • Start early when possible
  • Bring Someone with you when possible

Trail Etiquette

  • Stay on marked trails
  • Pack out all trash
  • Respect wildlife
  • Yield appropriately on trails
  • Keep noise levels low
  • Leave nature as you found it
  • More info can be found at Scouting Hiking Safety
